\section{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:setup}

\paragraph{Benchmark 1: verbal multiword expressions.}
PARSEME annotates verbal multiword expressions (VMWEs): possibly discontinuous token sets with a category such as verbal idiom, light-verb or verb--particle construction \citep{ramisch2018parseme,ramisch2020parseme}. A Chinese VMWE is often one token, such as a verb with its complement (\zht{推出}) or a four-character idiom. We use Chinese from edition 1.2 (train 35,326 sentences; test 3,462 sentences, 786 VMWEs) and English from edition 1.1, the last with English (train 3,471; test 3,965 sentences, 501 VMWEs). The procedure reads the lemmas, parts of speech and dependency relations supplied with the data. Batches come from the training file (three sentences with a VMWE, two without); the development set is 150 sentences.

\paragraph{Benchmark 2: Uniform Meaning Representation.}
\umr \citep{gysel2021design,bonn2025umr2} represents a sentence as a rooted graph of concepts linked by roles, with attributes such as aspect and polarity, plus document-level relations. For Chinese we use the 31 gold documents available to us (712 sentences), preprocessed as in \citet{chun2024pipeline}, with training documents from 0001--0014 and development documents from 0027--0029. Experiments were run in stages, so there are three test sets: T1 = 0004--0005 (99 sentences) and T2 = 0005--0007 (157) for fragment mode, and H = 0015--0018, 0021--0024 (138) for closed mode. H is excluded from reflection and tuning, but the authors have inspected it across versions, so comparisons on H are retrospective \exper{E1}. For English we use the native annotation of \umr 2.0, with batches from the guideline's examples and a narrative, 25 development sentences, and 143 test sentences from news and narrative text, not used before the final runs. Appendix~\ref{app:exposure} lists the gold each version has seen.

\paragraph{Procedures.}
$R_0$ is the hand-written fragment seed and $R_{xy}$ the version after round $y$ on training document $x$. The closed seed $J_0$ was tuned by hand on 0001--0003; $J_2$ follows unmeasured reflection on 0001--0002, and $K_1$--$K_4$ continue from $K_0=J_2$ with measured reflection on 0008--0011. In the batch runs, $\Pi_r$ is the version after $r$ rounds. The PARSEME seeds were written before any training sentence was read (Appendix~\ref{app:parseme}). For \umr we run batches from $J_0$ and from a revision of $J_2$ whose questions moved into data files and whose inventories were completed, including all 27 aspect values; its $\Pi_0$ restricts aspect to the seven values of the training gold and writes event negation as \texttt{:polarity -} (\S\ref{sec:analysis}). English starts from the Chinese $\Pi_0$.

\paragraph{Models and baselines.}
Fragment decisions are made by Claude Sonnet 5.5, DeepSeek V4.1 Flash, Qwen3.5-9B and Qwen3.8-27B \citep{qwen3}; closed decisions by Jev \citep{typesafe2026jev}, the OpenAI Decisions API \citep{openai2026decisions} and Microsoft-Decision-1 \citep{microsoft2026decision}, which share one protocol. The document-mode reflector is Claude Opus 5.5; the batch and routing runs use Jev 1.13, Claude Sonnet 5.5 as reflector and Claude Haiku 5.5 for routing. Baselines are a \emph{single call} per sentence with a pointer to the guidelines, a \emph{detailed single call} describing the target format, and \emph{inventory + ten gold sentences}, in which Haiku writes the annotation given the category or concept inventory and the first ten pool sentences with their gold, a sixth of what batch reflection reads. For PARSEME we add the shared-task systems, re-scored from their released predictions, and the best published English result we know of \citep{rohanian2019bridging}. Further baselines are pending \exper{E2}.

\paragraph{Metrics.}
PARSEME: the official MWE-based F1 (exact tokens and category) and its restriction to VMWEs unseen in training, computed with the official script for all systems; with the released data our unseen counts differ from the published ones (Appendix~\ref{app:exposure}), so unseen F is comparable only within our tables. \umr: AnCast++ \citep{sun2024ancast} sentence-graph F (\F) and the comprehensive score (Comp.); batch and routing runs stop before the document wave and report \F. Each configuration was run once; a cold re-run changes \F by up to 0.6 in closed mode and by 2.3 for Qwen3.5-9B in fragment mode \exper{E3}.
