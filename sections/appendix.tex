\section{Data details}
\label{app:data}

\paragraph{\umr.} Splits are frozen by \texttt{code/splits.py} (seed 20261012) in \texttt{experiment/orchestration-v2/data/}. Chinese training: one batch of five consecutive sentences from each of ten training documents among \texttt{chinese\_umr-0001}--\texttt{0018}; Chinese test: 20 chunks of five consecutive sentences from the 13 blind-test documents with gold (\texttt{0021}--\texttt{0024}, \texttt{0027}--\texttt{0035}), at least one chunk per document. English training: five, three and two batches from \texttt{english\_umr-0004}, \texttt{0578} and \texttt{0579}; English test: all 95 sentences of \texttt{english\_umr-0005}--\texttt{0008} in 20 chunks. Input tokens are the gold \texttt{Words} lines, as in the shared task. Gold is rewritten in the strict format the official scorer reads (one graph per sentence, alignments restricted to graph variables, document-level relations restricted to the chunk). Scoring: \texttt{compare\_umr.pl --tsv}, with and without \texttt{--no-document-level}.

\paragraph{PARSEME.} Chinese: PARSEME 1.2 \texttt{ZH}; English: PARSEME 1.1 \texttt{EN}. Training batches: three sentences with at least one VMWE and two without, ten batches. Test: 50 sentences with and 50 without a VMWE, drawn from the official test file. Unseen F uses a training file reduced to the sentences with a VMWE, which gives the same seen/unseen split as the full file and runs in seconds. The shared-task systems' released predictions are re-scored on the same 100 sentences with the official \texttt{evaluate.py}.

\paragraph{Guidelines.} The \umr guidelines (\texttt{umr4nlp/umr-guidelines}, \texttt{guidelines.md}, 366k characters) and the PARSEME 1.2 guidelines (335k characters) are given to the baseline in full and were the only source of the seeds.
