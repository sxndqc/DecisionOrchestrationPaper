\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{Diagnosability.}
A guideline in context fails silently: nothing in GPT-6.1-sol's output says which part of the 366k-character guideline it misread. Each error of a procedure has an address (a step, its input, its options and their probabilities), so it can be found and fixed where it arises. The Haiku seed's English concepts for Chinese words, Jev's unlemmatized English verbs and the closed PARSEME seeds' spurious light-verb constructions (\S\ref{sec:case}) were each found in one column of the judge's output and traced to one file. The same addresses let the reflector read the decisions behind each error, and its edits are diffs that an annotator can review, keep or revert.

\paragraph{Decision models are cheap and promising.}
Decision models return probabilities over options that code has listed, which makes them the cheapest deciders we tried: about \$0.1--0.3 per hundred \umr sentences, against \$1.9 for Haiku and \$7.1 for GPT-6.1-sol with the guideline (Chinese). Closed parses are fast enough to measure every edit, which keeps closed-mode reflection from drifting, and Jev gives the best \umr procedure in Chinese. Their ceiling is set by the code around them: a decision model cannot pick an option that is not offered. Better enumerators raise that ceiling for every decision model at once, and the transfer result (\S\ref{sec:res-calib}) suggests that a learned procedure can move between decision models.

\paragraph{What reflection can and cannot do.}
\label{sec:disc-rsi}
With five gold sentences a round, reflection writes the conventions a guideline leaves implicit (Chinese concepts keep the source word; \zht{有} is not a light verb) and fixes procedural errors, with the largest gains in the first rounds. Three limits are visible. (1) Unmeasured fragment edits drift (Haiku's Chinese PARSEME procedure loses almost ten points after round 3); measuring them would need cheap fragment decisions. (2) The training batches are tiny, so the gap between training and test grows and threshold refitting overfits; more gold strengthens both checks. (3) Reflection edits what exists and rarely changes the decomposition itself, which is where English PARSEME, close to what frontier models already know, stays below the guideline in context.

\paragraph{When to decompose.}
The frontier model in context is strongest where the scheme is close to what such models know and the output is short (English verbal MWEs); decomposition is strongest where the output is structure and the conventions are particular (Chinese \umr). For a new scheme or language early in a project, a procedure costs a few dollars to run, improves from the first batches of gold, and leaves a trace in which every error can be found.
